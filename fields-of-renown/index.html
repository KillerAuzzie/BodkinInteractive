The opening

Shipwrecked

Every journey to Gwynedd starts on a small island off its coast. It is the game's first chapter and its training ground. Every system you learn here comes with you to the mainland.

  1. I

    The storm

    A longboat through a night of thunder. Lightning strikes close, the sea takes the rest, and you wake face-down on a strange shore with nothing.

  2. II

    Survive the days

    Fish the shallows, keep warm by the fire, sleep through the dark, pray for a blessing. Earn coin and spend it at the island's store on torches and timber.

  3. III

    Learn the blade

    Directional sword fighting and the bow, against the island's wolves. Read the attack, pick your angle, mind your stamina.

  4. IV

    Escape day

    On the tenth day a wrecked longboat washes ashore, and the bandits come with it. Hold them off, repair the hull, and row out through the storm toward Gwynedd.

Beyond the island

The world

North Wales, thirteenth century. Opportunity and danger share the same road.

The island teaches you to survive. Gwynedd asks what you will do with it. Fish, farm, trade, and fight to carve out your place as a lone wanderer, a rising merchant, a sword for hire, or something in between. Seasons shift the land. Markets move. Alliances form and break. Renown is earned, not given.

Gameplay

What you'll do

Systems you meet on the island and carry into Gwynedd.

  • Directional combat

    Flick the mouse to choose your swing, block to match theirs. Every swing costs stamina, so timing beats spamming.

  • Archery

    Draw, hold, and loose. A bow for hunting and for thinning out a fight before it reaches you.

  • Survival

    Health, stamina, and the buffs that keep them up: Warm by a campfire, Well Rested after sleep, Blessed after prayer.

  • Weather & storms

    Clear skies turn to rain and full storms, with lightning and rolling thunder. The sea gets rougher the further out you go.

  • Boats & the sea

    Board, row, and steer longboats that ride the swell and run aground if you push them into the shallows.

  • Fishing

    Welsh waters for food and coin, and the quiet hours between storms.

  • Day & night

    A living clock. Nights are dark, so plant torches anywhere to push it back, and pick them up when you move on.

  • Skills

    A leather-bound skills book tracks what you've trained, what each skill does, and how far to the next level.

  • Trade & coin

    Earn it, spend it, save it. Coin buys the tools of survival now and the markets of Gwynedd later.
