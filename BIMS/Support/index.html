FAQs

Quick answers for common BIMS questions.

Where do I use BIMS?

The hosted app is at https://bims.bodkininteractive.com/. Product overview: bodkininteractive.com/BIMS.

How do I create an account?

Open Sign up, enter email and password, then confirm if prompted. Already registered? Use Sign in.

Can others edit my map?

Yes — via team invites or an edit share link. View links let people open the map without editing. Revoke links you no longer want active.

Do viewers need an account?

View-only share links can be opened without an account. Editors generally need a BIMS account and must sign in on the same hosted site.

Keyboard shortcuts?

Tab = child branch · Shift+Tab or Enter = sibling · Delete/Backspace = remove node · double-click = edit label · Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo.

How do I delete a map or my data?

Delete maps you own from the dashboard. For account deletion on the hosted service, email Support from your account address. See Privacy policy.
