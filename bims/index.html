Built for clear thinking

Everything you need to capture structure — then share it without the mess.

  • Live collaboration Edit together with presence. Teams and roles keep maps organized.
  • Fast branching Tab for a child, Shift+Tab or Enter for a sibling — maps stay keyboard-first.
  • Auto-layout Toggle ELK auto-adjust when you want structure without dragging forever.
  • Comments on nodes Threaded discussion sits on the idea it belongs to — not a separate chat dump.
  • Share links View or edit links for people outside your team when you need them.
  • Twenty board themes Light, dark, and themed boards so each map can feel like its own space.

How it works

Sign up, open a map, invite people — or share a link.

  1. Create

    Start a personal map or work inside a team.

  2. Branch

    Build the tree with keyboard shortcuts and drag when you want.

  3. Collaborate

    Comment on nodes, share view/edit links, keep everyone on the same board.
