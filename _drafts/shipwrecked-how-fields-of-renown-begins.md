Fields of Renown has always been about making a name for yourself in 13th-century Gwynedd. What's
changed is how you get there. The game now opens at sea.

## The storm

You start on a longboat in the middle of a storm. The sea is heaving, thunder rolls in from the
distance, and then lightning strikes close. Fade to black. You wake up face-down on the shore of a
small island off the Welsh coast with nothing.

## Why an island?

A medieval RPG has a lot of systems. Dropping someone into all of them at once is a lot. The island
gives us a contained place to teach each one properly before the mainland opens up:

- **Survival:** health, stamina, and buffs. *Warm* by a campfire, *Well Rested* after sleeping, *Blessed* after praying.
- **Combat:** directional sword fighting in the spirit of Mount & Blade. Flick the mouse to pick a swing,
  block to match, and watch your stamina, because every swing costs some.
- **Archery:** for hunting and for the fights you'd rather not let reach you.
- **Night:** the island gets properly dark. Torches from the store can be planted anywhere and picked back up.
- **Weather:** clear, overcast, rain, and full storms, with lightning and thunder that arrives later the further away the strike is.
- **Skills:** a small leather-bound book that shows what you've trained and how far you are from the next level.

## Escape day

On the tenth day a wrecked longboat washes up near where you came ashore, and the bandits come with it.
Survive them, buy the timber to repair the hull, and row out through the storm. That's the way to Gwynedd.

## What's next

We're polishing combat feel and the weather before the next round of playtests. If you want in,
